# Institutional Evidence

## Johns Hopkins SIS replacement
https://provost.jhu.edu/ssei/sis-replacement-project/
Describes information silos and friction in retrieval and workflows. Design implication: authoritative data ownership and integration boundaries.

## SIS implementation challenges
Mukerjee (2012), Student information systems: implementation challenges and the road ahead.
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1360080X.2012.642332
Discusses institution-specific implementation complexity. Keep the lab prototype scoped and document assumptions.

## Waitlist policy examples
Vanderbilt: https://www.vanderbilt.edu/enrollmentbulletin/registration-essentials/waitlist-process/
Explains sequential processing and blockers such as schedule conflicts, credit limits and reserved seats.

UT Austin: https://registrar.utexas.edu/schedules/082/registration
Useful example of explaining that a waitlist does not guarantee enrollment.

## Privacy reference
US Department of Education FERPA: https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa
Useful privacy-by-design reference in its US context. Do not present FERPA as Indian law.

## Evidence limits
Institutional sources describe specific contexts. Reddit is anecdotal. Technical docs explain mechanisms but do not prove our implementation works. Validate with tests.
